# Real-world acceptance checklist

These require actual people, accounts and operational evidence. Leave them open
until completed; passing fixtures must not be substituted.

- [ ] Interview three stall owners and two office coordinators; record observed
      order, delivery and credit problems without committing personal data.
- [ ] Obtain two concrete paid-pilot commitments and agree office ordering,
      access, delivery, settlement and support terms.
- [ ] Configure actual transactional email and verify delivery privately.
- [ ] Complete real Razorpay Test checkout/capture, cancellation, refund,
      webhook replay and recovery using the agreed per-stall accounts.
- [ ] Obtain provider/financial review for advance liability, top-up confirmation,
      refund handling, reward budgets, fraud review and reconciliation.
- [ ] Confirm owner approval and revocation, messages, order states, foreground
      sound and poor-network retries on the actual target phones.
- [ ] Check offers, recurring consent, pause/cancel, holidays, cutoff/window,
      price changes, capacity and insufficient funds with the pilot participants.
- [ ] Configure reviewed HTTPS/session controls, recovery/support ownership,
      monitoring/alerts, secure scheduled backups and successful restore drills.
- [ ] Complete an independent release/security review and measured load test.
- [ ] Reconcile pilot orders, refunds, credit/advance accounts, rewards, recorded
      expenses and provider/bank records; resolve every unexplained difference.
- [ ] Collect the actual agreed app fee and measure paid repeat offices,
      contribution assumptions, owner effort, service reliability and support load.
- [ ] Approve each commercial phase exit using that evidence before expansion.
